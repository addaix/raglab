raglab.judging#

Graded rubric judging — every candidate gets a level, three axes and evidence (raglab#8).

A binary relevant/not cannot say “mentions the word but is not about it”. The evaluator built by make_graded_rubric_evaluator() asks an injected judge, for each candidate in the round’s pool, for the three Self-RAG axes (is_about, is_supported, is_useful), a level on an ordered scale and a verbatim evidence_quote from the candidate’s disclosed content. The judge proposes; deterministic gates here dispose:

  • a level above unsupported_cap needs is_supported — topical vocabulary without supporting content is capped (the direct fix for on-word distractors);

  • with require_evidence_quote, it also needs a quote found verbatim in the content the judge was shown (whitespace-insensitive);

  • calibration_offset (levels, learned from a gold slice) shifts every level down (positive: the judge over-grades) or up;

  • the level options are shown in a fresh random order on every call, against position bias.

The result is a Judgement whose additive graded map carries every label, whose relevant keeps the candidates at or above relevant_level, and whose back-edge comes from an injected refine policy (none by default: the loop stops). As in raglab.llm, the default judge is built lazily on oa, so import raglab stays offline, and a judge that fails on a candidate leaves it ungraded rather than guessing.

The level a judge’s proposal is allowed, after calibration and the gates.

The quote must be at least min_quote_chars long and found in content, ignoring case and runs of whitespace (judges re-case and re-wrap what they copy). calibration_offset is in levels, rounded half up, so a shift is the same at every level.

>>> label = GradedLabel('core', True, False, True, 'a DAG of tasks')
>>> apply_gates(label, 'Runs a DAG of tasks asynchronously.').level
'tangential'
>>> ok = GradedLabel('core', True, True, True, 'graph  library')
>>> apply_gates(ok, 'A graph library.').level, apply_gates(ok, 'Nothing here.').level
('core', 'tangential')
>>> apply_gates(ok, 'A graph library.', calibration_offset=1.4).level
'strong'

Evaluator

Every candidate is judged once per goal: one judge call per (source, artifact) the first time it appears in a pool for that goal, its label remembered for later rounds, so a kept hit is not re-judged (nor lost to a later failing call). Judgement.graded is keyed by artifact_id; an id found in several sources carries its best label. A reply that does not parse leaves the candidate ungraded (logged); when every judge call of a round raises, the first error is re-raised rather than returning an empty, “sufficient” round. This evaluator is for labelling a pool, not for cheap filtering.
